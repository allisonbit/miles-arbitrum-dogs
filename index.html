Questions

Before you buy.

What is $MILES?

A meme coin on Arbitrum One named after Miles, an English Springer Spaniel. It is a token about a dog. It does not do anything else, and it does not claim to.

Why Arbitrum and not Solana or Base?

Because that is the chain Miles sits next to. Arbitrum One also settles to Ethereum, confirms in well under a second, and costs a fraction of a cent to trade on — which matters when the whole point is that anyone can join in.

How do I make sure I have the right token?

Only ever use the contract address at the top of this page or from the official Telegram and X accounts linked in the footer. Copycat tokens with the same name and image appear within hours of any launch. Check the address character by character before you swap.

Is there a tax, a whitelist, or a presale?

No, no, and no. Zero buy and sell tax, no allocation held back for the team, and no presale round. Liquidity was added at launch and the LP tokens were burned.

Is Miles okay with all of this?

He is asleep on the rug. He has no idea. He would like you to know that there is a tennis ball under the sofa and nobody will get it out for him.
